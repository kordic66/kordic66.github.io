About 9A4KJ O 9A4KJ

9A4KJ is the callsign of Davor Kordic, an electrical engineer and amateur radio operator based in Croatia. This site documents decades of radio experiments, hardware projects and technical knowledge. 9A4KJ je pozivni znak Davora Kordića, elektrotehničkog inženjera i radioamatera iz Hrvatske. Ova stranica dokumentira desetljeća radio eksperimenata, hardverskih projekata i tehničkog znanja.

Areas of interest include SDR (Software Defined Radio), antenna construction, Arduino-based automation, and CW (Morse code) operation. Područja interesa uključuju SDR (Software Defined Radio), gradnju antena, automatizaciju temeljenu na Arduinu i CW (Morseov kod) rad.

Active on HF, VHF, and regular participant in international radio contests. Aktivan na KV, VHF i redoviti sudionik međunarodnih radio natjecanja.
